About the iZombie Quiz

Think you know iZombie? This 50-question quiz tests how closely you remember the characters, zombies, brains, organizations, relationships and memorable events from the series. It is designed for viewers who want to test their knowledge of the strange world created around Liv Moore and the people connected to her.

Questions can cover Liv Moore, Ravi Chakrabarti, Clive Babineaux, Blaine DeBeers and other important characters. You may also encounter questions about zombie rules, brains, businesses, groups, identities, occupations, locations and events that shaped the story.

Some questions focus on major storylines, while others test smaller details that may be easy to forget. The quiz is therefore intended for both regular viewers and fans who remember the more unusual details of iZombie. Your final score shows how much of the series you can still recall.

How the iZombie Quiz Works

The quiz contains 50 multiple-choice questions. Each question has four possible choices, with one correct answer. Read each question carefully and select the answer you believe is correct before moving forward.

Every correct answer is worth 1 point, giving you a maximum possible score of 50 points. Your final score is used to determine your iZombie Knowledge level. A higher score means you successfully remembered more of the characters, events, zombie details and other information covered by the quiz.

The quiz is intended as entertainment and fan trivia, rather than an official assessment of how well someone knows the series. If you do not get the score you wanted, you can take the quiz again and see whether you can improve your result.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ to begin the challenge. The quiz displays your current question number and a progress bar so you can keep track of your position through all 50 questions.

Choose an answer and use Next to continue through the quiz. The Back button can be used to return to an earlier question when available. Take your time with questions that involve character identities, relationships, organizations or specific events.

After completing the quiz, your result screen displays your final score and iZombie Knowledge level. You can select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz, use SHARE MY RESULT to share your performance, or choose CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to see whether another iZombie fan can beat your score.

iZombie Trivia and FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 50 questions, and every question has four possible answers.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 50 points because each correct answer is worth 1 point.

What does the quiz cover?

Questions cover characters, zombies, brains, groups, businesses, occupations, identities, relationships, locations and memorable events from iZombie.

Is this a general zombie survival quiz?

No. This is primarily a knowledge and trivia quiz about iZombie. The questions are based on the show's characters, story, world and fictional zombie rules.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and attempt to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS option after completing the quiz and compare your iZombie knowledge with other fans of the series.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for iZombie. Questions may reveal important characters, identities, relationships, zombie-related developments, organizations, locations and major events from the series.

If you have not finished watching the show, consider completing it before taking the quiz to avoid having important story details revealed.
