About The iZombie Quiz

Think you know everything about iZombie? Put your knowledge to the test with this 50-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover characters, zombies, brains, groups, occupations, real names, businesses and memorable events from the television series.

Each correct answer gives you 1 point, giving you a maximum possible score of 50.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the iZombie quiz work?

You will face 50 questions about iZombie. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 50 points. Every correct answer is worth 1 point.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember iZombie, including its characters, zombies, groups, businesses and major events.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
